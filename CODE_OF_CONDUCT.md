# Code of Conduct

This project is a small, friendly place to build a useful app. Everyone taking part, in issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions, is expected to:

- be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new
- criticise ideas and code, never people
- assume good intent, and ask before assuming the worst
- keep discussions on topic

Not acceptable: harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, personal attacks, sharing someone's private information, spam, and repeated off-topic or bad-faith comments.

## Food and health

This is a food log, and food can be a sensitive subject. Don't comment on anyone's body, weight or diet, and don't give medical advice. Nothing in this project is medical advice.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or delete comments, close or lock issues and pull requests, and block people from the repository and the Untethered Citizens organization, at their discretion. To report a problem privately, use the contact details at [untetheredcalories.com/support](https://untetheredcalories.com/support/).
